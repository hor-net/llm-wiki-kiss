# On-call rotation

Policy describing how on-call rotations are scheduled, escalated and
handed over across teams.

## Rules

- Each service has a primary and a secondary on-call contact.
- Rotation runs weekly, starting Monday 09:00 in the team's local time.
- Handover happens in the team's on-call channel with a written summary
  of active incidents.

## Escalation

1. Page the primary on-call (5 minutes).
2. Page the secondary on-call (10 minutes).
3. Page the team's manager (15 minutes).
4. Open a major incident and notify the leadership channel.

## Compensation

- On-call duty is compensated per the company's policy.
- Incidents handled outside business hours are tracked for time-off.
